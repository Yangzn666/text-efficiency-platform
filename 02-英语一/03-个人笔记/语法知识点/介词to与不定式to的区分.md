# 介词to与不定式to的区分

## 核心要点

**to 作为介词时，后面加 doing（动名词）**  
**to 作为不定式符号时，后面加 do（动词原形）**

---

## 判断方法

### 关键规则
- 如果前面有 **be + 形容词/过去分词**，**to** 通常是**介词** → 后面加 **doing**
- 如果表示**目的**或**将来动作**，**to** 是**不定式符号** → 后面加 **do**

---

## be limited to + doing

### 含义
局限于做某事、仅限于做某事

### 结构
```
be limited to + doing（动名词）
```

### 例句
> "This means that our noses are **limited to perceiving** those smells which float through the air..."  
> 这意味着我们的鼻子只能感知飘浮在空气中的气味...

- ✅ **limited to perceiving** - 正确
- ❌ limited to perceive - 错误

---

## 同类固定搭配（to是介词，后接doing）

| 固定搭配 | 含义 | 示例 |
|---------|------|------|
| **be committed to doing** | 致力于做 | He is committed to **improving** his skills. |
| **be dedicated to doing** | 献身于做 | She is dedicated to **helping** others. |
| **be used to doing** | 习惯于做 | I'm used to **working** late. |
| **be accustomed to doing** | 习惯于做 | He's accustomed to **getting** up early. |
| **look forward to doing** | 期待做 | I look forward to **seeing** you. |
| **object to doing** | 反对做 | They object to **changing** the plan. |
| **be opposed to doing** | 反对做 | She is opposed to **raising** taxes. |
| **be addicted to doing** | 沉迷于做 | He is addicted to **playing** games. |
| **be reduced to doing** | 沦落到做 | He was reduced to **begging** for food. |
| **confess to doing** | 承认做了 | He confessed to **stealing** the money. |
| **devote to doing** | 投身于做 | She devoted her life to **teaching**. |

## to是不定式的情况（后接do）

### 常见搭配

| 固定搭配 | 含义 | 示例 |
|---------|------|------|
| **want to do** | 想要做 | I want to **learn** English. |
| **hope to do** | 希望做 | She hopes to **pass** the exam. |
| **decide to do** | 决定做 | They decided to **go** abroad. |
| **plan to do** | 计划做 | He plans to **study** hard. |
| **promise to do** | 承诺做 | I promise to **help** you. |
| **agree to do** | 同意做 | She agreed to **join** us. |
| **refuse to do** | 拒绝做 | He refused to **answer**. |
| **learn to do** | 学习做 | They learned to **swim**. |
| **manage to do** | 设法做到 | She managed to **finish** on time. |
| **afford to do** | 负担得起 | I can't afford to **buy** it. |

## 记忆技巧

### 1. 看to前面的词
- **形容词/分词 + to** → 通常是介词 → doing  
  例：be used to, be limited to, be committed to
  
- **动词 + to** → 通常是不定式 → do  
  例：want to, hope to, decide to

### 2. 理解语义
- **介词to**：表示"对于、关于、向"等关系 → doing  
  例：I'm used to **the situation** → I'm used to **dealing** with it.
  
- **不定式to**：表示"去做、将来要做" → do  
  例：I want **to learn** English.（将来要学的动作）

### 3. 替换测试
如果可以替换成 **for** 或其他介词，说明to是介词 → doing  
- I'm used to **the cold** = I'm used to **cold weather** → doing  
- ❌ I'm used to **be cold**（错误）

## 真题示例

### 2005年完型填空第3题
> This means that our noses are **___ to perceiving** those smells which float through the air...

**选项**：
- A. limited ✅
- B. committed
- C. dedicated
- D. confined

**解析**：
- limited to doing - 局限于做（正确）
- committed to doing - 致力于做（语义不符）
- dedicated to doing - 献身于做（语义不符）
- confined to doing - 局限于（多指空间限制）

**句意**：这意味着我们的鼻子只能感知飘浮在空气中的气味...

---

## 易错提醒

### ❌ 常见错误
1. **be used to do** - 错误！应该是 **be used to doing**（习惯于做）
2. **look forward to do** - 错误！应该是 **look forward to doing**（期待做）
3. **be committed to do** - 错误！应该是 **be committed to doing**（致力于做）

### ✅ 正确对比
- I **used to smoke**.（过去常常抽烟 - used to do）
- I **am used to smoking**.（我习惯了抽烟 - be used to doing）

- I **want to go**.（我想去 - 不定式）
- I **am used to going**.（我习惯了去 - 介词+doing）

---

## 总结

记住这个公式：
```
be + 形容词/分词 + to + doing（介词用法）
动词 + to + do（不定式用法）
```

**核心词汇**：be limited to doing - 局限于做
